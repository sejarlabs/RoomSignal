Privacy Policy

App Name: RoomSignal

Effective Date: October 24, 2023

1. Introduction

Welcome to RoomSignal. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how RoomSignal handles your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Data Collection & Local Storage

RoomSignal is designed from the ground up as a 100% offline-first and privacy-first application:

  • No Cloud Servers: We do not operate remote servers or cloud databases to collect your data.
  • Local SQLite Storage: All floor plans, survey measurements, and settings are stored strictly and securely in a local database on your device.
  • No Accounts: No user registration, login, or account creation is required to use the app.

3. Permissions Explained

To provide wireless analysis functionality, RoomSignal requests the following Android permissions:

  • Location Access (Fine & Coarse): Required by the Android operating system to scan nearby Wi-Fi RSSI signal strengths and frequency bands. Your physical geographic coordinates are never collected, transmitted, or shared.
  • Wi-Fi & Network State: Used to read currently connected Wi-Fi SSID, link speed, and connection status.

4. Third-Party Services & Analytics

RoomSignal contains no third-party analytics, no tracking SDKs, and no advertising networks. Your network data never leaves your device.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our developer support channel.
